Project Leader · 2025

NeuroLima addresses something most people in Indonesia do not talk about: dementia. I initiated it to connect awareness work with real care home support. Managed stakeholder coordination, non-profit budgeting, and corporate outreach.

Alzheimer Indonesia
Volunteer · 2025

Volunteered with Alzheimer Indonesia running psychological support sessions for elderly residents and organizing community events. The stigma around dementia in Indonesia is real - most of the work was making people comfortable enough to talk about it.

Three awareness events in one month, building a broad community network.
