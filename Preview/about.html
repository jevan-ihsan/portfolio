I’m a first-year Commerce student at the University of Toronto, specialising in Finance with a deep interest in strategy, consulting, and technology. I'm energized by fast-paced environments and love collaborating with people who challenge me to think bigger.

Beyond finance and spreadsheets, I care about social impact. I believe good work requires thoughtful collaboration, empathy, and a commitment to projects that actually help the community. I’m always open to new learning opportunities, challenges, and interesting conversations.
